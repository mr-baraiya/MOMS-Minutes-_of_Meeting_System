Minutes of Meeting (MOM) Management SystemIntroductionThe Minutes of Meeting (MOM) Management System is a web-based application designed to automate the process of managing meetings, recording minutes, tracking attendance, and maintaining meeting-related documents in an organized manner.Problem StatementManual meeting management leads to data inconsistency, difficulty in tracking attendance, loss of documents, and inefficiency. A centralized system is required to overcome these issues.ObjectivesTo automate the process of scheduling and managing meetings within an organization.To provide a centralized platform for recording and storing Minutes of Meeting (MOM) in a structured and secure manner.To enable accurate and efficient tracking of attendance for all meeting participants.To reduce manual paperwork and minimize errors associated with traditional meeting management methods.To support role-based access control for Admin, Meeting Organizer (Convener), and Staff users.To allow uploading, viewing, and downloading of meeting-related documents and MOM files.To generate meeting-wise and summary reports for better monitoring and decision-making.To improve transparency and accountability by maintaining a complete history of meetings and decisions.To enhance productivity by simplifying communication and follow-up through recorded action items and decisions.To ensure data consistency, integrity, and easy retrieval of meeting information.ScopeThe system provides functionality to create, edit, and cancel meetings in a structured and organized manner. It supports attendance management by allowing meeting organizers to add participants and mark their presence digitally.The system allows uploading, viewing, and downloading of Minutes of Meeting (MOM) documents and related files.It maintains a centralized repository for all meeting records, attendance data, and documents.The system generates meeting-wise and summary reports for analysis and record keeping.Role-based access control ensures that users can access features according to their assigned roles.The system improves transparency, efficiency, and traceability of meeting-related activities.UsersAdmin	System management and maintenanceMaster data management (Meeting Types, Departments, Venues, Staff)View all meetings, attendance, and reportsUser access control and system configurationMeeting Organizer (Convener)Conduct and manage meetingsCreate, edit, and cancel meetingsAdd participants and mark attendanceUpload MOM documents and related filesView meeting-wise and summary reportsStaffView assigned meetings and detailsCheck attendance statusView and download MOM documentsLimited access based on assigned permissionsFunctional RequirementsUser LoginThe system shall allow users to log in securely using valid credentials and provide access based on their assigned roles.Role-Based Access ControlThe system shall restrict functionalities according to user roles such as Admin, Meeting Organizer, and Staff.Meeting Creation, Editing, and CancellationThe system shall allow authorized users to create new meetings, modify existing meeting details, and cancel meetings with appropriate reasons.Participant ManagementThe system shall allow meeting organizers to add or remove participants for a meeting.Attendance MarkingThe system shall provide functionality to mark and store attendance of participants for each meeting.Minutes of Meeting (MOM) ManagementThe system shall allow uploading, viewing, and downloading of MOM documents and related files.Document Upload and ManagementThe system shall support secure uploading and management of meeting-related documents.Meeting ViewingThe system shall allow users to view meeting schedules, details, and past meeting records.Report GenerationThe system shall generate meeting-wise and summary reports related to meetings and attendance.User Profile ManagementThe system shall allow users to view and update their profile details.Non-Functional RequirementsSecurityThe system shall ensure secure authentication, role-based access control, and protection of sensitive meeting and user data.PerformanceThe system shall provide fast response times for user actions such as meeting retrieval, attendance marking, and document access.ScalabilityThe system shall be capable of handling an increasing number of users, meetings, and records without performance degradation.UsabilityThe system shall provide a user-friendly interface that is easy to understand and use for all types of users.Use Case IdentificationCommon Use Cases (All Users)LoginLogoutView DashboardView ProfileAdmin Use CasesManage UsersManage Roles & PermissionsManage Meeting
